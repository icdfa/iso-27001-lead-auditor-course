# ISO/IEC 27001 Auditing Practical Guide

This guide serves as a quick reference for Lead Auditors conducting ISO/IEC 27001 audits in the field.

## Chapter 1: The Lead Auditor Mindset
A successful Lead Auditor balances technical knowledge with professional skepticism. You must maintain independence, rely only on objective evidence, and avoid making assumptions based on the auditee's reputation. Your role is to assess conformity against the standard, not to act as a consultant designing their security architecture.

## Chapter 2: Pre-Audit Preparation Checklist
Before arriving on-site for a Stage 2 audit, ensure you have completed the following:
- Reviewed the Stage 1 report and noted areas of concern.
- Confirmed the audit scope and objectives with the client.
- Developed a risk-based Audit Plan organized by process/department, not just by clause number.
- Communicated the Audit Plan to the auditee and confirmed interviewee availability.
- Prepared tailored working documents (checklists) based on the organization's SoA and risk profile.

## Chapter 3: Master Audit Checklist (Key Clauses)

| Clause | Key Audit Questions | Evidence to Seek |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **4.1 Context** | How does the organization monitor external threats and internal changes? | PESTLE/SWOT analysis, management meeting minutes. |
| **5.1 Leadership** | How does top management demonstrate commitment to the ISMS? | Budget approvals, strategic alignment documents, interview with CEO/CISO. |
| **6.1.2 Risk Assessment** | Is the risk assessment methodology applied consistently? | Risk assessment procedure, populated risk register. |
| **6.1.3 Risk Treatment** | Does the SoA accurately reflect the implemented controls? | Statement of Applicability, Risk Treatment Plan with owner sign-offs. |
| **7.2 Competence** | How is the competence of key security personnel ensured? | Training records, certifications, job descriptions. |
| **8.1 Operations** | Are security processes executed as planned, including outsourced processes? | Process documentation, vendor SLAs, SLA monitoring reports. |
| **9.2 Internal Audit** | Is the internal audit program impartial and risk-based? | Audit schedule, auditor independence verification, internal audit reports. |
| **9.3 Management Review** | Does top management actively review ISMS performance data? | Meeting minutes showing discussion of metrics, risks, and resource allocation. |
| **10.1 Corrective Action** | Do corrective actions address the root cause to prevent recurrence? | Nonconformity logs, root cause analysis records, verification of implemented fixes. |

## Chapter 4: Conducting Effective Interviews
Use the **OSCE Framework**:
- **Open:** Establish rapport and explain the purpose.
- **Show Me:** Ask open-ended questions and request demonstrations.
- **Check:** Verify statements by requesting objective evidence (records, logs).
- **End:** Summarize findings and thank the interviewee.

## Chapter 5: Writing Findings (The PLO Structure)
Always use the PLO structure for nonconformities to ensure they are undeniable:
1. **Provision:** Cite the specific requirement (ISO 27001 clause or internal policy).
2. **Location:** Specify exactly where the failure occurred.
3. **Objective Evidence:** State the verifiable facts demonstrating the failure.

## Chapter 6: Evaluating Corrective Action Plans (CAPs)
Apply the Five-Question Test to every submitted CAP:
1. Does it include an immediate correction?
2. Has a valid root cause analysis been performed?
3. Does the corrective action specifically address the root cause?
4. Is responsibility assigned?
5. Is the timeline realistic?
