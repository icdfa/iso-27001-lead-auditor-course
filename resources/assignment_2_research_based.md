# Assignment 2: Research-Based Critical Analysis

## Overview
**Type:** Academic Research Report
**Marks:** 100
**Word Count:** 3,500-5,000 words
**Time:** 8-12 hours

## Task Description
Select a major, publicly documented information security breach from the past five years (e.g., the Equifax breach, the SolarWinds supply chain attack, or the Capital One breach). Produce a critical, evidence-based analytical report examining the failure through the lens of ISO/IEC 27001:2022.

## Report Structure and Marking Scheme

### Section 1: Introduction and Background (10 Marks)
- Provide a brief overview of the chosen organization.
- Describe the nature of the breach, the timeline of events, and the type of data compromised.

### Section 2: Critical Analysis of the Failure (30 Marks)
- Analyze the root causes of the breach (technical, procedural, and human factors).
- Discuss the impact on stakeholders (customers, shareholders, regulators).
- Evaluate the legal and financial consequences for the organization.

### Section 3: ISO/IEC 27001 Gap Analysis (30 Marks)
- Identify at least **5 specific clauses** (from Clauses 4-10) of ISO/IEC 27001 that the organization failed to implement or maintain effectively, leading to the breach. Justify your choices with evidence from public reports.
- Identify at least **2 specific Annex A controls** that were either missing or ineffective.

### Section 4: Proposed ISMS Implementation Strategy (20 Marks)
- Propose a high-level, 18-month strategy for the organization to implement a robust ISMS compliant with ISO/IEC 27001 to prevent recurrence.
- Outline key milestones, resource requirements, and leadership responsibilities.

### Section 5: Critical Evaluation of the Standard (10 Marks)
- Critically evaluate whether strict compliance with ISO/IEC 27001 alone would have been sufficient to prevent this specific breach. Are there limitations to the standard?

## Submission Guidelines
- Use professional, academic language.
- Cite all sources using a standard referencing format (e.g., APA, Harvard).
- Ensure the report is well-structured with clear headings and subheadings.
