# Course Syllabus: ISO/IEC 27001 Lead Auditor Training

## 1. Course Overview
This self-paced training course provides comprehensive preparation for the ISO/IEC 27001 Lead Auditor certification. It covers the requirements of the ISO/IEC 27001:2022 standard, the auditing principles of ISO 19011:2018, and practical auditing techniques.

## 2. Target Audience
- Auditors seeking formal ISO/IEC 27001 Lead Auditor certification.
- Information Security Managers (CISOs, ISMs) responsible for ISMS implementation.
- IT and Risk Management professionals.
- Compliance and Governance officers.

## 3. Course Objectives
Upon completion of this course, participants will be able to:
- Understand the core principles of information security and the ISMS framework.
- Interpret the requirements of ISO/IEC 27001:2022 from an auditor's perspective.
- Plan, conduct, report, and follow up on an ISMS audit in accordance with ISO 19011.
- Apply risk-based auditing techniques and gather objective evidence.
- Write clear, professional nonconformity reports.

## 4. Course Structure
The course consists of 10 comprehensive reading modules and several practical resources:

- **Module 1:** Introduction to Information Security and ISO/IEC 27001
- **Module 2:** Fundamental Concepts of the ISMS (Clauses 4-5)
- **Module 3:** Planning and Risk Management (Clause 6)
- **Module 4:** Support and Operation of the ISMS (Clauses 7-8)
- **Module 5:** Performance Evaluation and Improvement (Clauses 9-10)
- **Module 6:** Fundamental Audit Concepts and Principles (ISO 19011)
- **Module 7:** Preparing for an ISO/IEC 27001 Audit
- **Module 8:** Conducting an ISO/IEC 27001 Audit
- **Module 9:** Closing the Audit and Follow-up
- **Module 10:** Case Studies and Practice Exam

## 5. Assessment
Participants should complete the Practice Exam in Module 10 and the two assignments provided in the `resources` directory to test their readiness for formal certification.
